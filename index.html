Research

  1. 2026 · with Cantay Caliskan, Nyaradzo Mararanje, Albert Mejooli, Zhihan Zhang

    What Street-Level Imagery Can and Cannot Tell Us about Campus Sustainability

    Can you see a university's sustainability in pictures of its campus? Across 1.84M images of 252 U.S. campuses, dozens of models could not reproduce the AASHE STARS and QS ratings. We built the Visible Campus Environment Index instead, which measures only what a camera can credibly see. Read the paper · See the route

  2. 2026 · working paper with Cantay Caliskan

    Legopolitics: Toying with the Enemy

    A scene-level study of LEGO-style propaganda from the 2026 Iran–U.S.–Israel war: 363 videos, 7,288 coded scenes and 96,530 frames, compared with Iranian English-language state media. Read the paper · See the route

  3. Summer 2026 · SICSS, with Peng-Ting Kuo and Gaeun Jung

    You In or Out? Character Rhetoric and Audience Response in Attack on Titan

    We paired dialogue from all four seasons with 170,000+ Reddit comments and used LLMs to score rhetoric. Identity-based rhetoric drove polarization, and solidarity appeals landed well only from protagonists.

  4. 2024 to 2026 · Rochester Human-Computer Interaction Lab

    SOPHIE: a standardized online patient

    An LLM-based patient that helps healthcare professionals practice end-of-life conversations. I recruited 50+ study participants and analyzed the implementation.

NYC 311 Signal

An automated daily pipeline and dashboard over every NYC 311 request since January 2025: 6.9M requests and 8M+ change records. It keeps an exact history of each request and scores the chance a new one stays open past seven days.

GitHub Tableau Public view

Result: the delay model reaches 0.71 PR-AUC on 313K later requests it never saw, ahead of three baselines. It adds only a little over each complaint type's historical rate, so most of the signal is the complaint type itself.
